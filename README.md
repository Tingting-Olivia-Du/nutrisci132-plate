# MyPlate Around the World

A virtual gallery by Tingting Du
NUTR SCI 132, Fall 2026 - Participation Activity

Live page: https://tingting-olivia-du.github.io/nutrisci132-plate/

A participation activity for NUTR SCI 132. I took a typical meal from two cuisines and sorted
the foods into the five MyPlate food groups to see how well they line up.

- Room 1: Korea - bibimbap, banchan, kimchi, tofu, doenjang jjigae
- Room 2: Mexico - tacos, corn tortillas, beans, pico de gallo, mango, queso fresco

Both meals cover most of the five groups already. Dairy is the group that does not really
transfer, and the main health watch-outs are sodium in the Korean meal and frying and sugary
drinks in the Mexican one.
